Before you take the next step.

A few helpful things to know before you get started.

Still curious? Get in touch →
What does a career gap analysis do?

It helps you compare the experience and skills you describe with the requirements of a role you are considering, so you can identify strengths and possible areas to develop.

Do I need to know my next role already?

Having a target role gives you the most focused comparison, but you can explore different directions and see how your current strengths may transfer.

Will an analysis decide whether I'm qualified?

No. A skills comparison is a planning aid, not a hiring decision or a measure of your potential. You bring context and judgment that no checklist can replace.

Is my CV analyzed by AI?

This project is currently a frontend demo. It does not upload, extract, or analyze real CVs, and it does not provide AI-generated career advice.
